Mae Ngat Dam Floating Raft Day

Longtail across Mae Ngat reservoir to a floating raft house to swim, kayak and lunch on fresh fish

  • Free cancellation 24 h
  • Hotel pick-up
  • Licensed cooperative drivers
  • Drivers receive 60%

Highlights

  • A longtail boat ride across a mountain-ringed lake
  • Floating raft restaurants serving fresh fish and local dishes
  • Swimming or kayaking from the raft, where offered
  • Set your own return time with the boatman

Programme

  1. 08:30Hotel pick-up
  2. 10:00Mae Ngat Somboon Chon Dam viewpoint
  3. 10:30Pier: longtail boat out to the rafts
  4. 11:00Floating raft: swim, kayak, relax
  5. 12:00Lunch on the raft
  6. 14:30Boat back to the pier
  7. 16:30Back to your hotel

Drivers do not act as tour guides. A licensed guide can be added when you book.

Included

  • Pick-up and drop-off within the service area
  • Your driver waits at every stop on the programme
  • Drinking water and a multilingual route card
  • Compulsory vehicle insurance
  • Free cancellation up to 24 hours before

Paid on site

Sri Lanna National Park entry at posted rates · Boat, food and kayaks paid directly to the raft or boatman; agree prices before boarding · Wear a life jacket on the water · Water quality varies, so swim only where the raft advises · Bring swimwear, a towel and sunscreen

Best time

Nov–Feb for cool breezes and a calm lake · Mar–May for cooling off in the water · Weekdays are far quieter than holidays
