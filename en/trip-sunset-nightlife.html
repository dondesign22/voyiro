Sky-High Sunset & Live Jazz

Sunset from a 22nd-floor sky bar, Tha Phae Gate after dark, then live jazz by the North Gate

  • Free cancellation 24 h
  • Hotel pick-up
  • Licensed cooperative drivers
  • Price locked when you confirm

Highlights

  • Mai The Sky Bar on the 22nd floor, over the old city and the Ping River
  • Tha Phae Gate by night, Chiang Mai's classic photo spot
  • Live music nightly at North Gate Jazz Co-Op, with a jam session on Tuesdays
  • Your driver takes you home, so you can enjoy a drink without a care

Programme

  1. 17:00Hotel pick-up
  2. 17:20Mai The Sky Bar, 22nd floor of Meliá Chiang Mai, for sunset
  3. 18:45Tha Phae Gate after dark, photo stop
  4. 19:15Chang Phueak Gate night market for a light bite
  5. 19:45North Gate Jazz Co-Op, opposite Chang Phueak Gate, live music
  6. 20:30Back to your hotel

Drivers do not act as tour guides. A licensed guide can be added when you book.

Included

  • Pick-up and drop-off within the service area

  • Free cancellation up to 24 hours before

Paid on site

Food and drinks paid on site · Non-hotel guests at Mai The Sky Bar buy an entry ticket redeemable against drinks, at posted rates · Bar opens 16:00 Oct–Feb and 18:00 Mar–Sep (pick-up moves to 17:45 in those months) · No cover charge at North Gate; support the musicians by ordering drinks

Best time

Clearest skies Oct–Feb; sunset ranges from about 17:50 (Dec) to 19:05 (Jun) · Avoid the hazy Mar–Apr burning season · Tuesday jam night is the liveliest
