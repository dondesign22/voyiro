About voyiro

Built in Thailand for local travel across ASEAN

We believe ride-hailing technology should make local public drivers stronger — not make them disappear. We're starting in Thailand and expanding to neighbouring countries.

Our storyMissionValuesTeamRoadmapNewsCareers
Our story

The same question in every tourist city

Why do travellers have to haggle over fares when licensed public drivers are waiting all over town? And why are passengers turning to apps that don't use public transport at all?

The same question comes up in Thailand and in neighbouring countries. voyiro was created to connect three groups in the same city — transport cooperatives, local tourism businesses and passengers — so they all benefit openly, under one standard across the whole network.

Mission

Make local travel easy, safe and fair for everyone involved

Vision

To be the local public ride network that works across Thailand and ASEAN

Our promise

Clear fares upfront, verified drivers, income that stays in the city

Our values

What we will never compromise on

Compliance before growth

We only expand where we can do it properly, even if it's slower.

Drivers are partners

Drivers always receive the largest share of the fare, and are represented by their cooperatives.

Transparent to the last baht

Everyone can see where the money from each trip goes.

Money that circulates locally

Income flows back to drivers, cooperatives and local businesses.

Thailand and ASEAN roadmap

Growing one area at a time, the right way

Phase 1 · Launch area

Set up systems and standards with cooperatives, hotels and restaurants in Chiang Mai [Pending: ช่วงเวลา เช่น ไตรมาส 4/2569]

Phase 2 · Northern Thailand

Expand cooperatives and partners across the north, such as Chiang Rai [Pending: ช่วงเวลา]

Phase 3 · Every region of Thailand

Central, Eastern, Western, Southern and Northeastern Thailand — for example Pattaya, Hua Hin and Phuket [Pending: ช่วงเวลา]
