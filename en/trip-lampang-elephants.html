Lampang Elephants, No Riding

Watch elephants bathe, visit hospitals caring for elephants from across Thailand, then browse a forest market

  • Free cancellation 24 h
  • Hotel pick-up
  • Licensed cooperative drivers
  • Price locked when you confirm

Highlights

  • Thai Elephant Conservation Center, a government-run centre for Thai elephants
  • Elephant bathing time, on the centre's daily schedule
  • Elephant hospitals treating sick and injured elephants from across Thailand
  • Kad Thung Kwian: chilli pastes, northern food and seasonal forest produce

Programme

  1. 07:30Hotel pick-up
  2. 09:00Thai Elephant Conservation Center, Hang Chat
  3. 09:30Elephant bathing (per the day's schedule)
  4. 10:30The centre's elephant hospital
  5. 11:30Friends of the Asian Elephant hospital (if open to visitors)
  6. 12:30Kad Thung Kwian: lunch and local produce
  7. 14:00Depart
  8. 15:30Back to your hotel

Drivers do not act as tour guides. A licensed guide can be added when you book.

Paid on site

Centre entry at posted rates (Thai and foreigner rates differ) · The centre also sells rides and shows; neither is part of this trip, as we watch elephants without riding · The Friends of the Asian Elephant hospital relies on donations · May close on special days such as Thai Elephant Day (13 Mar) or during royal visits

Best time

Year-round · Arrive before 10:00 to catch bathing time (weekday and weekend times differ) · Nov–Feb is coolest
