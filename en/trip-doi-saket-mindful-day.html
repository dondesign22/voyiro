Mindful Day: Yoga & Meditation in Doi Saket

A full day of meditation, yoga and a home-cooked vegetarian lunch at a garden retreat among the rice fields

  • Free cancellation 24 h
  • Hotel pick-up
  • Licensed cooperative drivers
  • Price locked when you confirm

Highlights

  • Morning walking and sitting meditation (30–60 min)
  • A walk through the paddies to learn the rice-growing cycle
  • Level-adjusted yoga, then restorative yoga in the afternoon
  • Wat Phra That Doi Saket and a local market

Programme

  1. 07:30Hotel pick-up
  2. 08:15Arrive at the garden retreat in Choeng Doi
  3. 08:30Meditation and rice-field walk
  4. 10:00Morning yoga
  5. 11:30Vegetarian lunch
  6. 13:00Wat Phra That Doi Saket and local market
  7. 15:00Meditation / restorative yoga
  8. 17:00Programme ends
  9. 17:30Back to your hotel
