Ethical Elephant Day in Mae Taeng

A full day watching elephants roam freely at a no-riding, no-show sanctuary in the Mae Taeng valley

  • Free cancellation 24 h
  • Hotel pick-up
  • Licensed cooperative drivers
  • Price locked when you confirm

Highlights

  • Elephants living naturally: no riding, no performances
  • Rescue stories shared by the sanctuary's own staff
  • Mae Taeng River and forested hills all along the way
  • Door-to-gate transfer, with your driver waiting to bring you back

Programme

  1. 07:30Hotel pick-up
  2. 09:00Arrive and check in at your booked sanctuary
  3. 09:30Sanctuary morning programme: observe and walk with the herd
  4. 12:00Lunch as part of the sanctuary programme
  5. 13:00Sanctuary afternoon programme
  6. 15:00Depart, with a rest stop in Mae Taeng
  7. 16:30Back to your hotel

Drivers do not act as tour guides. A licensed guide can be added when you book.

Included

  • Pick-up and drop-off within the service area
  • Your driver waits at every stop on the programme
  • Drinking water and a multilingual route card
  • Compulsory vehicle insurance
  • Free cancellation up to 24 hours before

Paid on site

Programme fee paid directly to the sanctuary; book ahead and confirm it accepts self-arriving guests (some sell transport-inclusive packages only) · Choose only no-riding, no-show sanctuaries with no painting or football · Sanctuary staff run the programme; your driver is not a guide · Wear clothes that can get dirty and comfortable walking shoes

Best time

Year-round · Nov–Feb is coolest · Jun–Oct paths turn muddy, so bring shoes that can get wet
