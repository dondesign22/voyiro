Mae Ya Falls & Phra That Si Chom Thong

Honour the Buddha relic at Wat Phra That Si Chom Thong, then a short walk to the 260 m tiers of Mae Ya Falls

  • Free cancellation 24 h
  • Hotel pick-up
  • Licensed cooperative drivers
  • Price locked when you confirm

Highlights

  • Wat Phra That Si Chom Thong Worawihan, a venerable Lanna temple
  • A Buddha relic brought out for worship on major holy days
  • Mae Ya Waterfall, about 30 tiers dropping roughly 260 m
  • A short, nearly flat walk suitable for all ages

Programme

  1. 08:00Hotel pick-up
  2. 09:30Wat Phra That Si Chom Thong Worawihan
  3. 10:45Mae Ya Waterfall, walk to the base
  4. 12:30Lunch in Chom Thong
  5. 13:30Chom Thong market and local snacks
  6. 15:00Back to your hotel

Drivers do not act as tour guides. A licensed guide can be added when you book.

  • Free cancellation up to 24 hours before

Paid on site

Temple free (donations welcome); dress modestly and remove shoes in the viharn · Mae Ya lies within Doi Inthanon National Park, entry at posted rates at its own gate (published figures differ), open about 08:00–17:00 · Small car park that fills fast on holidays

Best time

Jun–Nov when the falls spread nearly 100 m wide · Dec–Feb for lower water, clearer rock tiers and cool air
