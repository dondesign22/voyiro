Hill-Tribe Textiles at Warorot

Handwoven cloth, Hmong embroidery and northern cotton at Warorot, the Hmong alley and Chinatown shops

  • Free cancellation 24 h
  • Hotel pick-up
  • Licensed cooperative drivers
  • Price locked when you confirm

Highlights

  • Warorot's upper floors: hill-tribe textiles, cotton and fabric by the length
  • Trok Lao Jo Hmong market: embroidery, bags and hemp cloth
  • Bolt-fabric shops around Chinatown and the Pung Tao Gong shrine
  • Bargaining welcome; heavy buys ride in the vehicle

Programme

  1. 09:00Hotel pick-up
  2. 09:15Warorot Market (Kad Luang), upper-floor textile zone
  3. 10:00Trok Lao Jo Hmong market: embroidery and hill-tribe crafts
  4. 10:40Fabric shops around Warorot, Chinatown
  5. 11:05Pung Tao Gong shrine
  6. 11:30Back to your hotel

Drivers do not act as tour guides. A licensed guide can be added when you book.
