Legal compliance

Three layers of compliance

voyiro does not accept private vehicles, and does not accept drivers without a public driving licence.

Layer 1 · Driver and vehicle

Licensed under the law

Drivers hold a public driving licence, vehicles are registered under the correct category, and compulsory motor insurance and road tax are fully paid, as required by Thailand's motor vehicle and land transport laws.

Layer 2 · Cooperative

Member of a transport cooperative

The cooperative is a legal entity that oversees its members, checks their qualifications and is accountable for their conduct. Every driver must be endorsed by their cooperative before taking jobs.

Layer 3 · Platform

A second platform check

voyiro re-checks each driver's and vehicle's documents in the system, and suspends job assignment as soon as a document expires.

Laws that apply directly

  • From 31 March 2026, drivers taking jobs through an app must hold a public driving licence.
  • The deadline for full compliance is 30 September 2026.
  • Platforms are responsible for verifying their drivers' qualifications.
  • Personal data must be handled under the Personal Data Protection Act B.E. 2562 (2019) (PDPA).

voyiro is designed to accept only drivers who meet the conditions above, from day one.

Personal data

Personal data handled as the law requires

In Thailand we comply with the Personal Data Protection Act B.E. 2562 (2019) (PDPA).

  • We collect only the data needed for the trip and for safety.
  • We do not sell personal data to third parties.
  • You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your own data.
  • To exercise your data subject rights, call 081-960-3227, Mon–Sat 9am–5pm.
