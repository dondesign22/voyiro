Safety and compliance

Compliant from day one, verifiable on every trip

We start with drivers who already hold public driving licences, then add international-grade platform safety standards on top.

Legal compliance

Three layers of compliance

voyiro does not accept private vehicles or drivers without a public driving licence. In every country we operate in, we comply with that country's transport laws. [Pending: หน่วยงานกำกับดูแลและใบอนุญาตที่ต้องมีในแต่ละประเทศเป้าหมาย]

Layer 1 · Drivers and vehicles

Legal licensing

Drivers hold public driving licences, and vehicles are registered by type with compulsory insurance and road tax paid in full, in line with Thailand's Motor Vehicle Act and Land Transport Act.

Layer 2 · Cooperatives

Members of a transport cooperative

Cooperatives are legal entities that look after their members, verify qualifications and are responsible for member discipline.

Layer 3 · Platform

Platform standards

Application for platform certification submitted to the Department of Land Transport. [Pending: เลขที่หนังสือรับรอง / วันที่ยื่น / สถานะ]

Personal data

Personal data, handled according to the law

In Thailand we comply with the Personal Data Protection Act B.E. 2562 (2019), and with the data protection laws of every country we operate in.

  • We collect only the data needed for your trip and your safety
  • We never sell personal data to third parties
  • You can view, correct or delete your own data
