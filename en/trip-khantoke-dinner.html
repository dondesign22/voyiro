Khantoke Dinner & Lanna Dance

Transfers to a classic khantoke dinner: northern dishes on low trays and Lanna fingernail and sword dances

  • Free cancellation 24 h
  • Hotel pick-up
  • Licensed cooperative drivers
  • Drivers receive 60%

Highlights

  • Old Chiangmai Cultural Center on Wualai Road, staging khantoke since 1970
  • Or Khum Khantoke, whichever you have booked
  • Northern Thai dishes served on a pedestal tray, usually with refills
  • Fingernail, sword and Tai Lue dances in a single evening

Programme

  1. 18:00Hotel pick-up
  2. 18:20Arrive at Old Chiangmai Cultural Center or Khum Khantoke
  3. 18:30Khantoke dinner is served
  4. 19:15Traditional Lanna performances
  5. 21:00Your driver waits at the venue
  6. 21:30Back to your hotel

Drivers do not act as tour guides. A licensed guide can be added when you book.
