Inthanon Royal Projects & Khun Wang

Stroll the temperate gardens of Inthanon Royal Project, see Siriphum Falls, then cross over to Khun Wang

  • Free to cancel until a driver accepts
  • Hotel pick-up
  • Licensed cooperative drivers
  • See the price before you book
  • Pay the driver in cash

Highlights

  • Inthanon Royal Agricultural Station's flower gardens and highland crops
  • Siriphum Falls, twin ribbons pouring off the cliff behind the station
  • Khun Wang's road of wild Himalayan cherry, Thailand's 'sakura' (Jan)
  • Lunch built around Royal Project vegetables

Programme

  1. 07:00Hotel pick-up
  2. 09:00Inthanon Royal Agricultural Station gardens and plots
  3. 10:30Siriphum Waterfall
  4. 11:30Lunch featuring Royal Project produce
  5. 12:45Turn off at km 31 towards Khun Wang
  6. 13:30Royal Agricultural Research Center (Khun Wang)
  7. 15:00Head back
  8. 17:00Back to your hotel

Drivers do not act as tour guides.

The more of you, the better the value

Country songthaew with 2฿2,025 / person
Country songthaew with 5฿810 / person
Country songthaew with 10฿405 / person

Included and conditions

  • Private charter of the whole vehicle, no guide: up to 10 people in a songthaew, up to 3 in a tuk-tuk
  • Trip times are as shown for each trip
  • Pay the driver in cash
  • Hotel pickup and drop-off inside the old city moat; an extra fee applies outside the moat
  • Route and add-ons can be adjusted to suit you
  • Price does not include entry fees, meals or special activities (as listed)
  • Book ahead, especially for rafting and restaurants for large groups
  • Drinking water on board for every trip
  • Suitable for families, older travellers and every kind of visitor
  • No animal riding, shows or elephant bathing in our tours

Paid on site

Doi Inthanon National Park entry at posted rates (foreign adults 300 THB, children 150 THB) plus vehicle fee · Any Royal Project station fee at posted rates · Khun Wang open about 08:00–16:00; the 17 km access road is narrow and steep, jammed in blossom season, and in heavy rain your driver may skip it for safety · Bring warm layers

Best time

January for cherry blossom (timing varies each year) · Nov–Feb for the best temperate blooms · Rainy season is lush but slippery
