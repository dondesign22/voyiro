Our drivers

It starts with the drivers
the city already trusts

voyiro does not put private cars up against public vehicles. We bring technology to drivers who are already licensed, and let cooperatives look after their own members.

  • Public driving licenceDocuments checked twice — by the cooperative and by voyiro
  • Identity verified every dayName, photo and vehicle plate match the passenger's screen
  • Clear earnings on every jobDrivers see what they will earn before accepting every job, and can check their totals in the driver app

Fair income

Every baht of the fare
can be checked

Every trip leaves a record and a receipt. Each party sees its own share in its own account after signing in.

Drivers

See what they will earn before accepting every job, and their totals in the driver app

The home cooperative

Sees its members' jobs and the cooperative's share under its signed agreement, in the cooperative system

Partners

See the bookings that came from them and their own earnings, in the partner account

Passengers

See the final fare before confirming, locked once booked, with a receipt for every trip

Sign inFor driversFor partners

Revenue-share rates are private to each contracting party. They are set out in each agreement and shown only in the accounts of the people concerned. Any change is announced in advance and never affects confirmed jobs.
