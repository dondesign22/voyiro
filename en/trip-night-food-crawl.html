After-Dark Street Food Crawl

The Cowboy Hat Lady's braised pork, the Chiang Mai Gate night market and the flower market after dark

  • Free cancellation 24 h
  • Hotel pick-up
  • Licensed cooperative drivers
  • Drivers receive 60%

Highlights

  • Khao kha moo at Chang Phueak Gate, a stall made famous by Anthony Bourdain
  • Chiang Mai Gate night market, savoury and sweet in one place
  • Ton Lamyai flower market at its busiest after dark
  • Your driver waits at every stop, with no parking or haggling over fares

Programme

  1. 18:00Hotel pick-up
  2. 18:15Chang Phueak Gate night market: Cowboy Hat Lady's khao kha moo
  3. 19:00Chiang Mai Gate night market
  4. 19:50Ton Lamyai flower market by the Ping River
  5. 20:40Chinatown by night, photo stop at the Chinese gate
  6. 21:00Back to your hotel

Drivers do not act as tour guides. A licensed guide can be added when you book.
