Thai Cooking Class & Market Visit

Browse herbs at Somphet Market, ride to the cooking school you've booked, and get picked up after class

  • Free cancellation 24 h
  • Hotel pick-up
  • Licensed cooperative drivers
  • Price locked when you confirm

Highlights

  • Somphet Market, an old-city morning market full of Thai herbs
  • Drop-off at the city cooking school of your choice
  • Cook 4–5 Thai dishes following the school's own syllabus
  • Private pick-up after class, with no waiting for a shared shuttle

Programme

  1. 08:00Hotel pick-up
  2. 08:15Somphet Market: herbs, curry pastes and Thai greens
  3. 08:50Drop-off at your booked cooking school (in town)
  4. 09:00Cooking class (many schools include their own market tour)
  5. 13:30Your driver waits at the school
  6. 14:00Back to your hotel

Drivers do not act as tour guides. A licensed guide can be added when you book.

Included

  • Pick-up and drop-off within the service area
  • Your driver waits at every stop on the programme
  • Drinking water and a multilingual route card
  • Compulsory vehicle insurance
  • Free cancellation up to 24 hours before

Paid on site

Cooking class booked separately at the school's posted rates (not included in the vehicle price) · Somphet Market free · Times adjust to the school's sessions (most offer morning and afternoon classes)

Best time

Morning sessions, when markets are liveliest, any day · Book classes ahead in high season, Nov–Feb
