Mae Rim & Mon Cham Highlands

Mae Sa Waterfall, the panoramic Mon Cham ridge and the terraced fields of Nong Hoi Royal Project

  • Free cancellation 24 h
  • Hotel pick-up
  • Licensed cooperative drivers
  • Drivers receive 60%

Highlights

  • Mae Sa Waterfall, ten tiers set in shady forest
  • Mon Cham's ridge-top panoramas and cool mountain air
  • Terraced flower and temperate-crop plots of Nong Hoi Royal Project
  • Hotel pick-up, and your driver waits at every stop

Programme

  1. 08:00Hotel pick-up
  2. 08:45Mae Sa Waterfall, lower to middle tiers
  3. 10:30Mon Cham ridge viewpoints and flower terraces
  4. 12:00Lunch on Mon Cham
  5. 13:15Nong Hoi Royal Project strawberry and temperate-crop plots
  6. 14:30Café and local produce stop in the Mae Sa Valley
  7. 16:00Back to your hotel

Drivers do not act as tour guides. A licensed guide can be added when you book.

Included

  • Pick-up and drop-off within the service area
  • Your driver waits at every stop on the programme

  • Compulsory vehicle insurance
  • Free cancellation up to 24 hours before

Paid on site

Mae Sa Waterfall: national park entry at posted rates (foreign adults 100 THB) plus parking · Mon Cham viewpoints are free; some flower plots charge a small fee · Food and shopping paid on site · Bring a long-sleeve layer and non-slip shoes

Best time

Nov–Feb for cool air and misty mornings · Strawberries roughly Dec–Mar · Jun–Oct the falls are fullest but paths are slippery
