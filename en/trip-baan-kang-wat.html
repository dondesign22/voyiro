Baan Kang Wat Artist Village

A leafy artists' village on the city's west side: ceramic and textile studios, a bookshop and small cafés

  • Free cancellation 24 h
  • Hotel pick-up
  • Licensed cooperative drivers
  • Price locked when you confirm

Highlights

  • Artist studios and craft shops in a shady garden setting
  • Ceramics, textiles and leather goods by Chiang Mai makers
  • Small cafés and eateries within the village
  • Sunday morning market (pick-up time can be adjusted)

Programme

  1. 10:00Hotel pick-up
  2. 10:25Baan Kang Wat: studios and craft shops
  3. 11:40Bookshop and small workshops (when open)
  4. 12:00Coffee or a light lunch in the village
  5. 12:40Depart for the city
  6. 13:00Back to your hotel

Drivers do not act as tour guides. A licensed guide can be added when you book.
