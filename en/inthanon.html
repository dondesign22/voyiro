Yellow songthaew queue, Doi Inthanon

Up Doi Inthanon with the local queue
one vehicle, the whole group

Yellow songthaews from the Mae Klang Waterfall queue take you to the summit of Doi Inthanon, the highest point in Thailand. Choose from five packages, from the 5am sunrise run to an afternoon half day. The drivers are local and know the best time to be at each stop.

Five ways up Doi Inthanon

Every package leaves from the Mae Klang Waterfall queue and comes back down before evening. The earliest departure is 05:00, the latest is early afternoon.

05:00 only · about 7 hours

1. Summit sunrise, 5am

  • Leave the Mae Klang Waterfall temple queue at 05:00 to reach the summit before sunrise
  • Doi Inthanon summit, the highest point in Thailand
  • The twin royal pagodas, Naphamethinidon and Naphaphonphumisiri
  • Hmong market for cool-weather produce and souvenirs
  • Wachirathan Waterfall on the way down

The only package that reaches the summit for sunrise. You must be at the queue by 05:00.

08:00 · about 7 hours

2. Classic four highlights

  • Doi Inthanon summit, the highest point in Thailand
  • The twin royal pagodas
  • Hmong market
  • Wachirathan Waterfall
  • Good for families and small groups who would rather not start before dawn

A later start that still covers every main stop, back at the queue in the afternoon.

12:00 · about 5 hours

3. Afternoon half day

  • Doi Inthanon summit
  • The twin royal pagodas
  • Hmong market
  • Back at the queue before evening

For travellers with half a day, or who arrive in Chiang Mai in the morning.

07:00 · about 8 hours

4. Nature route with Mae Ya Waterfall

  • All four highlights on Doi Inthanon
  • A stop at Mae Ya Waterfall, the largest in the national park
  • More time at each stop
  • Good for photography and easy walking

Mae Ya Waterfall is added to the route for an extra charge based on distance.

06:00 · about 8 hours

5. Khun Wang cherry blossom (seasonal)

  • Wild Himalayan cherry blossom at Khun Wang, December to February
  • Doi Inthanon summit
  • The twin royal pagodas
  • Hmong market

Runs only while the blossom is out. Check with the queue before booking ahead.

Prices and booking

Charter the whole vehicle, price agreed before you go

Whole vehicle

From THB 1,800 per trip, up to 10 passengers — about THB 180 each.

Smaller groups

Outside the high season the queue can run with 8 passengers. Ask the queue before you travel.

Book ahead, save 10%

Only a limited number of vehicles run each day. Book ahead for a 10% discount and the departure time you want.

These are starting prices per vehicle. They exclude the Doi Inthanon National Park entry fee and admission at individual sites, which are paid on the day at the published rates. The final price is confirmed with the queue before departure.

Add what you like

Want another stop on the way? Just ask the driver

  • Khun Wang cherry blossom, December to February
  • Mae Ya Waterfall, the largest in the national park
  • Viewpoints and coffee stops on the way up
  • Other stops along the Mae Klang Waterfall to summit route

Stops off the main route cost extra depending on distance. Agree it with the driver or the queue before you set off.

Boarding point: the Mae Klang Waterfall queue, Chom Thong

Arrange your time with the queue in advance and board at the queue. For the sunrise run you must be there by 05:00.
