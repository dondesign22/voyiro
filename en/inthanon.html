Yellow songthaew, Doi Inthanon

Up Doi Inthanon with local drivers
share a seat or charter the vehicle

Reach the highest point in Thailand in a local yellow songthaew. A shared seat is THB 180 per person, a yellow songthaew charter from Chom Thong is THB 1,800 per vehicle, or pick a full-day trip with hotel pick-up in town. Every price is the same as in the voyiro app.

Yellow songthaew

Share a seat, or charter the whole vehicle

The same prices as in the voyiro app, as of October 2026

Shared seat · departs 05:00

Shared yellow songthaew up Doi Inthanon

THB 180 per person

  • Board at the Wat Nam Tok Mae Klang queue, departing 05:00
  • Four stops on the mountain
  • Return to the boarding point included
  • Departs as soon as 10 seats are taken (from 8 outside the high season)
  • National park fees not included
Whole vehicle · one way

Yellow songthaew charter, Chom Thong → Doi Inthanon

THB 1,800 per vehicle, up to 10 people

  • The price starts from Chom Thong, one way, not from the city centre
  • Your whole group rides together
  • National park fees not included
Whole vehicle · van

Van charter, Chom Thong → Doi Inthanon

THB 2,200 per vehicle, up to 10 people

  • Air-conditioned, easier for older travellers and rainy days
  • The price starts from Chom Thong, one way
  • National park fees not included

Doi Inthanon National Park fees and entry to individual sights are not included and are paid on site at the official rates · Pay the driver in cash · Free to cancel until a driver accepts

Full-day trips from town

A songthaew from your hotel up Doi Inthanon

Price per vehicle, up to 10 people, vehicle and driver included, no guide

Doi Inthanon, the Roof of Thailand

Stand on Thailand's highest point, visit the twin royal pagodas and Wachirathan Falls, and hike Kew Mae Pan

THB 4,300 per vehicle · up to 10 people

Inthanon Royal Projects & Khun Wang

Stroll the temperate gardens of Inthanon Royal Project, see Siriphum Falls, then cross over to Khun Wang

THB 4,050 per vehicle · up to 10 people

Doi Inthanon Sunrise at Kew Mae Pan

Leave at 04:00 for first light over the mist at Kew Mae Pan, then the summit and pagodas before the crowds

THB 4,300 per vehicle · up to 10 people

Where to board
