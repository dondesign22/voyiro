Yellow songthaew, Doi Inthanon

Up Doi Inthanon with local drivers
share a seat or charter the vehicle

Reach the highest point in Thailand in a local yellow songthaew. A shared seat is THB 180 per person, a yellow songthaew charter from Chom Thong is THB 1,800 per vehicle, or pick a full-day trip with hotel pick-up in town. Every price is the same as in the voyiro app.

Shared seat · departs 05:00

Shared yellow songthaew up Doi Inthanon

THB 180 per person

  • Board at the Wat Nam Tok Mae Klang queue, departing 05:00
  • Four stops on the mountain
  • Return to the boarding point included
  • Departs as soon as 10 seats are taken (from 8 outside the high season)
  • National park fees not included

Yellow songthaew charter, Chom Thong → Doi Inthanon

THB 1,800 per vehicle, up to 10 people

  • The price starts from Chom Thong, one way, not from the city centre
  • Your whole group rides together
  • National park fees not included

Van charter, Chom Thong → Doi Inthanon

THB 2,200 per vehicle, up to 10 people

  • Air-conditioned, easier for older travellers and rainy days
