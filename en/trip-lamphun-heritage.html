Lamphun & Hariphunchai Heritage

One of the north's oldest cities: the golden Hariphunchai stupa, a national museum and Wat Chamadevi

  • Free cancellation 24 h
  • Hotel pick-up
  • Licensed cooperative drivers
  • Price locked when you confirm

Highlights

  • Wat Phra That Hariphunchai, the birth-year stupa for the Rooster
  • Hariphunchai National Museum
  • Wat Chamadevi and its ancient stepped Ku Kut chedi
  • The avenue of giant yang-na trees on the old Chiang Mai–Lamphun road

Programme

  1. 08:30Hotel pick-up
  2. 09:00Yang-na tree avenue (on the way)
  3. 09:45Wat Phra That Hariphunchai
  4. 11:00Hariphunchai National Museum (Wed–Sun)
  5. 12:15Lunch in Lamphun
  6. 13:30Wat Chamadevi (Ku Kut)
  7. 14:30Depart
  8. 15:30Back to your hotel

Drivers do not act as tour guides. A licensed guide can be added when you book.
