Terms of Use

Terms of service for voyiro

voyiro is operated by DONAUS Co., Ltd., with its office at 299/2 Moo 1, Khiang Doi Road, Chang Phueak, Mueang, Chiang Mai 50300, Thailand. By using our website or services, you accept these terms.

voyiro's role

  • voyiro is an intermediary that connects passengers with public drivers who belong to transport cooperatives. It is not itself a transport operator.
  • The transport service is the responsibility of the driver and their cooperative under Thailand's motor vehicle and land transport laws.
  • We accept only drivers who hold a public driving licence, with vehicles registered under the correct category.

Users' responsibilities

  • Passengers: provide accurate trip details, pay the fare shown by the system, and do not use the service for unlawful purposes.
  • Drivers: keep your licence and vehicle documents current, provide service to the standards of your cooperative and of voyiro, and charge the price shown by the system.
  • Cooperatives: check qualifications and approve members before they join the system, and oversee member conduct.
  • Partners: book rides for your customers on the agreed terms, and receive your share based on the list of completed trips.

Fares, fees and cancellations

  • Fares are calculated from a base fare for the vehicle type plus a distance charge, and the price is shown before you tap to book. Conditions may vary by city, distance and time of day.
  • The platform fee deducted from drivers follows the published driver tiers.
  • A trip can be cancelled before the driver sets off to collect you. If there is a dispute about the fare, report it with the trip number so it can be checked together with the cooperative.

Account suspension, complaints and governing law

  • We may immediately suspend a driver from taking jobs when their documents expire, or when an incident affects passenger safety.
  • Complaints are sent to the driver's cooperative and to voyiro together with the trip evidence. Report them on 081-960-3227, Mon–Sat 9am–5pm.
  • These terms are governed by Thai law, and the Thai courts have jurisdiction.
Privacy Policy

Protection of personal data

DONAUS Co., Ltd. is the data controller for the voyiro service and handles personal data under the Personal Data Protection Act B.E. 2562 (2019) (PDPA).

Data we collect and why

  • Identity and contact details, such as name and phone number, to verify identity and to stay in contact during the trip.
  • Location and route data, to match vehicles, calculate fares and track trips for safety.
  • Trip and payment data, to issue receipts, calculate partner shares and investigate complaints.
  • Driver and vehicle documents, to verify qualifications as required by law.
  • We collect only the data that is necessary, and we do not sell personal data to third parties.

Disclosure and retention

  • We disclose only what is necessary to the driver's cooperative, to the partner who booked the ride for a customer, and to payment service providers.
  • We disclose data to government agencies where required by law, or where necessary to provide assistance in an emergency.
  • We keep data only as long as necessary for the stated purposes and for the period required by law, after which it is deleted or de-identified.

Data subject rights

  • You may request access to, a copy of, correction of, deletion of, or restriction of the use of your data, or object to its processing.
  • You may withdraw your consent where processing is based on consent.
  • To exercise your rights or ask about personal data, call 081-960-3227, Mon–Sat 9am–5pm, or contact the office at the address above.
Cookie Policy

How we use cookies on this website

The voyiro website currently uses only the cookies necessary for the site to work, such as remembering your chosen language.

  • We do not use cookies for advertising, and we do not track visitors' behaviour across other websites.
  • You can disable or delete cookies in your browser settings, though some functions may not work fully.
  • If we add analytics tools in future, we will update this policy and ask for your consent before using them.
