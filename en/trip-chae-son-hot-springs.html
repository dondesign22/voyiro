Chae Son Hot Springs Mountain Crossing

Cross the ridge into Lampang to boil eggs in hot springs, soak in mineral water and hike to Chae Son Falls

  • Free cancellation 24 h
  • Hotel pick-up
  • Licensed cooperative drivers
  • Drivers receive 60%

Highlights

  • Boil eggs in bamboo baskets in springs of around 73°C
  • Private mineral-bath huts beside the stream
  • A forest walk of about 1.5 km to tiered Chae Son Waterfall
  • Coffee stop in Mae Kampong on the way

Programme

  1. 08:00Hotel pick-up
  2. 09:15Coffee stop in Mae Kampong
  3. 10:30Chae Son National Park: hot springs & egg boiling
  4. 11:30Walk to Chae Son Waterfall
  5. 13:00Lunch in the park
  6. 14:00Private mineral-bath hut
  7. 15:30Depart
  8. 18:00Back to your hotel

Drivers do not act as tour guides. A licensed guide can be added when you book.

Paid on site

Park entry: foreign adults 200 / children 100 THB, Thai 40 / 20 THB, plus a vehicle fee (national park posted rates) · Mineral-bath huts 50 THB per person · Eggs and baskets sold at the springs · Bring a towel, a change of clothes and walking shoes

Best time

Nov–Feb, when a hot soak feels best · Jul–Oct the falls roar but the mountain road can be slippery, so check conditions · Avoid long holiday weekends for shorter bath queues
