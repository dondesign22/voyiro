Lampang Horse Carriages & Teak Heritage

Horse-carriage rides by the Wang River, a Burmese-built teak temple, a 116-pillar mansion and Wat Pongsanuk

  • Free cancellation 24 h
  • Hotel pick-up
  • Licensed cooperative drivers
  • Price locked when you confirm

Highlights

  • Lampang's signature horse carriages along the Wang River
  • Wat Si Rong Mueang, a teak viharn built by Burmese craftsmen
  • Ban Sao Nak, an 1895 teak house raised on 116 pillars
  • Wat Pongsanuk, UNESCO Asia-Pacific heritage Award of Merit (2008)

Programme

  1. 07:30Hotel pick-up
  2. 09:30Wat Phra Kaew Don Tao Suchadaram
  3. 10:45Wat Si Rong Mueang
  4. 12:00Lunch in Lampang
  5. 13:15Ban Sao Nak
  6. 14:15Wat Pongsanuk
  7. 15:15Horse-carriage ride through the old town
  8. 16:30Depart
  9. 18:30Back to your hotel

Drivers do not act as tour guides. A licensed guide can be added when you book.

  • Pick-up and drop-off within the service area

Paid on site

Carriages are priced per carriage (2–4 seats) at the province's posted rates by ride length · Wat Phra Kaew Don Tao: small foreigner entry fee · Wat Si Rong Mueang and Wat Pongsanuk by donation · Ban Sao Nak charges entry at posted rates; check opening days · Dress modestly

Best time

Year-round · Late-afternoon light is perfect for the carriage ride · Nov–Feb is coolest
