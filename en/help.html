Passengers

Booking a ride and paying the fare

How is the fare calculated?

It is a base fare for the vehicle type plus a per-kilometre distance charge, and the price is shown before you tap to book. Conditions may vary by city, distance and time of day.

I booked but no driver accepted

Try booking again, or choose another vehicle type serving the same area. During the pilot, some routes still have a limited number of vehicles.

The driver charged more than the price shown

You do not have to pay more than the price shown. Contact the help team with your trip number and we will look into it together with the driver's cooperative.

How can I pay?

You can pay in cash or by e-wallet, depending on the payment options available in each area.

I left something in the vehicle

Report the lost item from that trip's receipt, or call the help team with your trip number.

Drivers

Accounts and earnings

Who can sign up as a driver?

You need a public driving licence, a vehicle registered under the correct category, and membership of a cooperative that works with voyiro. The cooperative is the one that approves its members.

How much is the platform fee?

It depends on your driver tier: Bronze 12% · Silver 11% · Gold 10% · Platinum 9%. The more you drive, the lower the fee. During the pilot, samlor drivers pay no platform fee for the first three months.

Where can I see my earnings?

Daily earnings are shown in the driver app. If a figure does not match, check your account details in your profile first, then contact your cooperative or the voyiro team with your driver ID.

My account has been suspended

First check the expiry dates on your public driving licence and vehicle documents — the system automatically suspends job assignment when a document expires. If that is not the cause, contact your cooperative.

Changing vehicle or phone number

Tell your cooperative so the new documents can be checked. The system is updated once the cooperative approves.

Cooperatives

Using the cooperative system

What can a cooperative see in the system?

A real-time overview of operations, usage statistics by route and by area, and details of the members currently taking jobs.

Can we use the data for route planning?

Yes. The system summarises travel demand by route so the cooperative can plan new routes and manage its members' quality standards.

What does the cooperative have to do?

Check qualifications and approve the members who join the system, oversee member conduct, and handle complaints together with voyiro.

Partners

Your share and booking rides for customers

How much do partners earn?

In town, THB 30–50 per trip · Airport or out of town, THB 60–100 per trip · Tours and packages, THB 20 per trip.

What are the pilot terms?

No joining fee · 12-month agreement · 30 days' notice to cancel · Maximum outstanding balance THB 5,000.

My share does not match what I counted

Compare it with the trip list in your dashboard. The system counts only completed trips booked through your partner account.

Can you issue a tax invoice?

Yes. Tax invoices can be issued for the share paid to partners.
