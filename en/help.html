The driver charged more than the fare shown

Never pay more than the fare shown. Contact our help team with your trip number and we'll look into it with the driver's cooperative.

Drivers

Account and earnings

My payout didn't arrive on Thursday

Check the bank account number in your profile. If it's correct, contact your cooperative or the voyiro team with your driver ID.

My account has been suspended

Check your document expiry dates, or whether your outstanding cash balance is over 2,000 baht. If neither applies, contact your cooperative.

Changing vehicle or phone number

Let your cooperative know so they can check your new documents. The system will update once the cooperative approves.

Partners

QR codes and revenue share

Our QR sign is damaged or lost

Contact the partner team for a replacement. The old QR code will be deactivated and a new one issued.

Our revenue share doesn't match our own count

Compare it with the trip list in your dashboard. Only completed trips booked from your QR codes are counted.
