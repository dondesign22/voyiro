Doi Saket Temple & Hot Spring Retreat

A gentle day: a hilltop temple with 360° views, a lakeside pause, then a private mineral soak and massage

  • Free cancellation 24 h
  • Hotel pick-up
  • Licensed cooperative drivers
  • Price locked when you confirm

Highlights

  • Wat Phra That Doi Saket: naga stairway and 360° district views
  • Mae Kuang Udom Thara reservoir viewpoint
  • Doi Saket Hot Spring: free foot soak and private bathing rooms
  • An easy pace that suits older travellers

Programme

  1. 08:30Hotel pick-up
  2. 09:15Wat Phra That Doi Saket
  3. 10:30Mae Kuang Udom Thara reservoir
  4. 11:30Doi Saket Hot Spring: soak / massage
  5. 13:15Lunch at a rice-field café
  6. 14:30Depart
  7. 15:30Back to your hotel

Drivers do not act as tour guides. A licensed guide can be added when you book.

Best time
