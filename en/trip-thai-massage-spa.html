Lila Thai Massage Afternoon

Two hours of Thai massage at Lila, a social enterprise for women after prison release, then coffee

  • Free cancellation 24 h
  • Hotel pick-up
  • Licensed cooperative drivers
  • Drivers receive 60%

Highlights

  • Lila Thai Massage, founded by a former director of the Chiang Mai Women's Prison
  • Therapists trained in Thai massage, given a fresh start
  • Several old-city branches, with door-to-door transfer
  • Unwind afterwards with Akha Ama coffee near Wat Phra Singh

Programme

  1. 13:30Hotel pick-up
  2. 13:50Lila Thai Massage, old-city branch (Ratchadamnoen, Chedi Luang or Ratvithi)
  3. 14:00Thai or oil massage, about 2 hours
  4. 16:10Akha Ama Coffee, Phra Singh branch
  5. 16:50Depart for your hotel
  6. 17:00Back to your hotel

Drivers do not act as tour guides. A licensed guide can be added when you book.
