Private Red Truck Charter · Half Day

Hire a whole red songthaew for up to 10 people and explore Chiang Mai for half a day

  • Free to cancel until a driver accepts
  • Hotel pick-up
  • Licensed cooperative drivers
  • See the price before you book
  • Pay the driver in cash

Included and conditions

  • Private charter of the whole vehicle, no guide: up to 10 people in a songthaew, up to 3 in a tuk-tuk
  • Trip times are as shown for each trip
  • Pay the driver in cash
  • Hotel pickup and drop-off inside the old city moat; an extra fee applies outside the moat
  • Route and add-ons can be adjusted to suit you
  • Price does not include entry fees, meals or special activities (as listed)
  • Book ahead, especially for rafting and restaurants for large groups
  • Drinking water on board for every trip
  • Suitable for families, older travellers and every kind of visitor
  • No animal riding, shows or elephant bathing in our tours
