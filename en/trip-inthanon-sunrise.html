Doi Inthanon Sunrise at Kew Mae Pan

Leave at 04:00 for first light over the mist at Kew Mae Pan, then the summit and pagodas before the crowds

  • Free cancellation 24 h
  • Hotel pick-up
  • Licensed cooperative drivers
  • Price locked when you confirm

Highlights

  • First light over a sea of mist from the Kew Mae Pan ridge
  • Thailand's highest summit in the crisp early morning
  • The twin royal pagodas in soft morning light, still quiet
  • Back in town by mid-afternoon with time to rest

Programme

  1. 04:00Hotel pick-up
  2. 06:00Kew Mae Pan trail with a local guide for sunrise
  3. 08:30Summit and Ang Ka nature trail
  4. 09:30Breakfast and hot coffee
  5. 10:15The twin royal pagodas
  6. 11:30Wachirathan Falls
  7. 12:30Lunch in Chom Thong
  8. 15:00Back to your hotel

Drivers do not act as tour guides. A licensed guide can be added when you book.

  • Pick-up and drop-off within the service area
  • Your driver waits at every stop on the programme

  • Free cancellation up to 24 hours before

Paid on site

Doi Inthanon National Park entry at posted rates (foreign adults 300 THB, children 150 THB) plus vehicle fee · Kew Mae Pan local guide 200 THB per group of up to 10 · Runs only while Kew Mae Pan is open, Nov–May · Pre-dawn is very cold: bring a jacket, hat and torch · Thick fog can hide the sunrise

Best time

Nov–Feb for the clearest sea of mist; sunrise around 06:30–07:00 · Long weekends are packed, so pick a weekday if you can
