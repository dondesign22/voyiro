Chiang Mai Specialty Coffee Crawl

Three landmark coffee stops: a champion barista's Nimman bar, Akha hill-farm coffee and an old-city gem

  • Free cancellation 24 h
  • Hotel pick-up
  • Licensed cooperative drivers
  • Price locked when you confirm

Highlights

  • Ristr8to, the champion barista's café on Nimman Soi 3
  • Akha Ama Coffee, sourced from Akha hill-tribe farms
  • Graph Café, a tiny old-city spot devoted to single origins
  • Made for coffee lovers, with no parking hassle at any stop

Programme

  1. 08:30Hotel pick-up
  2. 08:50Ristr8to, Nimmanhaemin Soi 3
  3. 09:50Akha Ama Coffee, Phra Singh branch, Ratchadamnoen Road
  4. 10:40Graph Café, Ratvithi Road
  5. 11:30Back to your hotel

Drivers do not act as tour guides. A licensed guide can be added when you book.
