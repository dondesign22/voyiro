Old City Temples by Night

Wat Chedi Luang bathed in light, Wat Phra Singh after dark, the Three Kings and Tha Phae Gate on a cool night

Highlights

  • Wat Phra Singh's courtyards in the evening calm
  • The Three Kings Monument after dark
  • Tha Phae Gate and the illuminated moat
