Partner types

Built for businesses whose customers need to get somewhere next

Hotels and accommodation

  • QR signs at the front desk and in guest rooms
  • Reception staff book rides for guests via LINE
  • Guests don't need an app or have to haggle over fares
  • Less pressure on your hotel shuttle

Tour companies and agencies

  • Pre-book full-day vans and samlors for groups
  • Monthly invoicing [Pending: เงื่อนไขเครดิตและรอบวางบิลสำหรับบริษัททัวร์]
  • Fixed per-vehicle prices you can include in tour packages
  • Local drivers selected by the cooperative
  • One account to book rides in every city across our Thailand and ASEAN network

Restaurants, spas and nightlife venues

  • Book rides back to the hotel for customers when they're done
  • Customers stay longer, knowing they can get home easily
  • QR codes on tables or at the cashier
  • Earn a share of every trip booked from your venue's QR code

FAQ

Questions from partners

Do guests pay more because the hotel gets a share?

No. The partner share comes out of the same fare guests would pay if they booked themselves.

We already have a shuttle or regular drivers. Can we use both?

Yes. Use voyiro when your own vehicles are busy or when guests want to go somewhere else.

Are rides available 24 hours a day?

[Pending: ช่วงเวลาให้บริการจริงในแต่ละจังหวัด]

If a guest has a problem with a driver, who's responsible?

Let the voyiro team know right away. We'll coordinate with the driver's cooperative and report back within [Pending: เวลาตอบกลับข้อร้องเรียน].

We have several branches. Can we use one account?

Yes. Each branch gets its own QR codes and reports, with totals combined in one account.
