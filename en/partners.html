Partner types

Made for businesses whose customers travel on

Hotels and accommodation

  • Reception staff book rides for guests from the hotel's account
  • See the price before booking, with no haggling with the driver
  • Earn a share of every trip booked through the hotel account
  • Less pressure on the hotel's own shuttle

Tour companies and agencies

  • Book vans and samlors for full-day group charters in advance
  • A fixed charter price per vehicle that you can build into a tour package
  • Local drivers selected by their cooperative
  • A 20 THB per trip share on tours and packages
  • One account books vehicles in every city where the service is open

Restaurants, spas and entertainment venues

  • Book a ride home for customers after they've finished
  • Customers stay longer because they know they can get home
  • Drivers hold a public driving licence and the vehicles are properly registered
  • Earn a share of every trip your staff book for a customer

Frequently asked questions

Questions from partners

Do guests pay more because the hotel takes a share?

No. The partner share comes out of the same fare the guest would pay booking the ride themselves.

We already have a shuttle and regular drivers — can we use both?

Yes. voyiro works alongside them, for when your own vehicles are busy or a guest wants to go somewhere else.

How is the share paid, and can we check it?

Every trip is tied to your partner code. You can see the daily entries in the dashboard and you receive a monthly statement with a tax invoice as your payment record.

If a guest has a problem with a driver, who is responsible?

Tell the voyiro team straight away. We take it up with the driver's cooperative and report the outcome back to you.

We have several branches — can we use one account?

Yes. Each branch gets its own partner code and its own reports, with the totals rolled up into a single account.
