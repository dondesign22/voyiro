Frequently asked questions

Questions about trips

Why does a package cost more than an hourly charter?

A package has a set programme and timing, free cancellation up to 24 hours before and advance payment, and its price already includes the hotel's or seller's share. If you'd rather plan your own route, choose an hourly charter.

Is it more expensive through my hotel or an international booking site?

Booking direct, through your hotel or through a tour company costs the same. International booking sites are about 30% higher because their commission is included.

Is the driver also a guide?

No. Your driver takes you along the route and waits at every stop. Under Thai law, guided commentary must be given by a licensed guide, which you can add when you book.

Are entrance fees included?

No. You pay them on site at the actual price, with no mark-up.

Why do out-of-town trips use country songthaews?

Tuk-tuks and red trucks serve the city inside Ring Road 3. Mountain and out-of-town trips, such as Doi Inthanon, Mae Kampong and Mae Wang, use country songthaews that are licensed for longer routes and seat up to 12.

Can just one or two of us book?

Yes. The price is per vehicle, so you pay the same whether you fill every seat or not.
