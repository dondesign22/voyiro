Doi Khun Tan Ridge Hike & White Railway Bridge

Hike the forest ridges of Doi Khun Tan, then watch a train roll over the century-old Tha Chomphu White Bridge

  • Free cancellation 24 h
  • Hotel pick-up
  • Licensed cooperative drivers
  • Price locked when you confirm

Highlights

  • Ridge trail to the Yaw 1 and Yaw 2 viewpoints
  • Forest above the historic Khun Tan railway tunnel
  • Tha Chomphu White Bridge, a concrete arch railway bridge from the reign of King Rama VI
  • Photograph trains crossing slowly over the arches

Programme

  1. 07:00Hotel pick-up
  2. 08:30Doi Khun Tan National Park HQ
  3. 08:45Ridge hike to the Yaw 1–Yaw 2 viewpoints and back
  4. 13:15Lunch in Mae Tha
  5. 14:30Tha Chomphu White Bridge: train watching (check the timetable)
  6. 15:45Depart
  7. 17:00Back to your hotel

Drivers do not act as tour guides. A licensed guide can be added when you book.

Paid on site

Park entry: foreign adults 100 / children 50 THB, Thai 20 / 10 THB · The last stretch of park road is steep and partly unpaved and may be impassable in heavy rain · Never walk into the railway tunnel · The White Bridge is a live railway line, so stay clear of trains · Wear hiking shoes and carry 2 litres of water

Best time

Nov–Feb for cool air and dry trails · Mar–Apr is hot and hazy · Rainy-season trails are slippery and leechy
