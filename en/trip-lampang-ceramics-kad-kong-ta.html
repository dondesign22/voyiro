Rooster Bowls & Kad Kong Ta Night Market

Paint your own rooster bowl at a ceramics museum, then graze Kad Kong Ta walking street by the river at night

  • Free cancellation 24 h
  • Hotel pick-up
  • Licensed cooperative drivers
  • Price locked when you confirm

Highlights

  • Dhanabadee Ceramic Museum and the story of Lampang's rooster bowl
  • Paint-your-own bowl or mug workshop (book 24 hours ahead)
  • Wat Chedi Sao Lang and its twenty white stupas
  • Kad Kong Ta, a weekend walking street among old shophouses

Programme

  1. 10:00Hotel pick-up
  2. 12:00Lunch in Lampang
  3. 13:00Dhanabadee Ceramic Museum: production tour
  4. 14:00Rooster-bowl painting workshop (pre-booked)
  5. 15:45Wat Chedi Sao Lang
  6. 16:45Ratsadaphisek Bridge & the Wang riverside
  7. 17:15Kad Kong Ta walking street
  8. 20:00Depart
  9. 22:00Back to your hotel

Drivers do not act as tour guides. A licensed guide can be added when you book.

Paid on site

Saturdays and Sundays only (Kad Kong Ta runs roughly 17:00–21:00/22:00) · Museum, factory tour and workshop at posted rates; book the workshop at least 24 hours ahead · Cash is easiest at the market

Best time

Weekends year-round · Nov–Feb evenings are pleasantly cool · In heavy rain some stalls stay closed
