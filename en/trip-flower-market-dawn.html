Dawn at the Flower Market

Rise early for fresh blooms at Ton Lamyai, the Muang Mai wholesale market and Warorot as its stalls open

  • Free to cancel until a driver accepts
  • Hotel pick-up
  • Licensed cooperative drivers
  • See the price before you book
  • Pay the driver in cash

Highlights

  • Ton Lamyai flower market as the day's fresh flowers arrive
  • Muang Mai, the working wholesale fruit-and-vegetable market
  • Warorot at first light and the Pung Tao Gong shrine in Chinatown
  • Soft morning light and a side of the markets most visitors never see

Programme

  1. 05:30Hotel pick-up
  2. 05:45Ton Lamyai flower market by the Ping River
  3. 06:30Muang Mai wholesale fruit and vegetable market
  4. 07:15Warorot Market (Kad Luang) as breakfast stalls open
  5. 07:40Pung Tao Gong shrine, Chinatown photo stop
  6. 08:00Back to your hotel

Drivers do not act as tour guides.

The more of you, the better the value

Tuk-tuk with 2฿400 / person
Tuk-tuk with 3฿267 / person

Included and conditions

  • Private charter of the whole vehicle, no guide: up to 10 people in a songthaew, up to 3 in a tuk-tuk
  • Trip times are as shown for each trip
  • Pay the driver in cash
  • Hotel pickup and drop-off inside the old city moat; an extra fee applies outside the moat
  • Route and add-ons can be adjusted to suit you
  • Price does not include entry fees, meals or special activities (as listed)
  • Book ahead, especially for rafting and restaurants for large groups
  • Drinking water on board for every trip
  • Suitable for families, older travellers and every kind of visitor
  • No animal riding, shows or elephant bathing in our tours

Paid on site

Free entry · Food and flowers paid on site · Ton Lamyai and Muang Mai trade almost around the clock and are busiest from pre-dawn to about 08:00 · Warorot open daily, stalls open progressively from early morning

Best time

05:30–08:00, any day · Cool, soft-light mornings Nov–Feb · Especially lively in the days before Loi Krathong
