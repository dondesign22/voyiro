Mae Sapok Falls & Huai Manao Raft Lunch

Step behind the curtain of Mae Sapok Waterfall, then lunch on a floating raft at Huai Manao Reservoir

  • Free cancellation 24 h
  • Hotel pick-up
  • Licensed cooperative drivers
  • Price locked when you confirm

Highlights

  • Mae Sapok Waterfall, where you can step behind the falling water
  • The headwaters of the Mae Wang River, green and cool
  • Floating raft restaurants on hill-ringed Huai Manao Reservoir
  • Quiet Mae Wang back roads, far from the tour crowds

Programme

  1. 08:00Hotel pick-up
  2. 09:45Mae Sapok Waterfall, Mae Win
  3. 12:15Huai Manao Reservoir, lunch on a raft
  4. 14:00Relax and take in the lake views
  5. 15:00Market or souvenir stop in Mae Wang–San Pa Tong
  6. 16:00Back to your hotel

Drivers do not act as tour guides. A licensed guide can be added when you book.

Paid on site

Mae Sapok Waterfall reported free, open about 08:00–17:00 (subject to change, check on site) · Huai Manao: food and raft paid to the restaurant; swimming in the reservoir is not allowed · Rocks at the falls are very slippery, so wear strapped non-slip sandals and bring a change of clothes

Best time

Jul–Nov when the falls are full · Dec–Feb for cool, comfortable days · Avoid days of heavy rain (flash-flood risk)
