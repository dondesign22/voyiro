Thai Dress Photo Ride

Dress up in Thai or Lanna costume, then ride to Tha Phae Gate and three photogenic temples in morning light

  • Free cancellation 24 h
  • Hotel pick-up
  • Licensed cooperative drivers
  • Price locked when you confirm

Highlights

  • Outfit and hair styling at a dress studio
  • Tha Phae Gate and the old brick walls by the moat
  • Wat Chedi Luang and the all-teak hall of Wat Phan Tao
  • A finale at Wat Sri Suphan's silver chapel

Programme

  1. 08:30Dress studio: outfit and hair
  2. 09:30Tha Phae Gate
  3. 10:00Wat Chedi Luang
  4. 10:50Wat Phan Tao, the teak hall
  5. 11:20Wat Sri Suphan
  6. 12:30Back to the studio

Drivers do not act as tour guides. A licensed guide can be added when you book.
