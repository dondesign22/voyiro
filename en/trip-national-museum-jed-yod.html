National Museum & Wat Chet Yot

Trace Lanna from prehistory onward at the National Museum, then visit the seven-spired Wat Chet Yot

  • Free cancellation 24 h
  • Hotel pick-up
  • Licensed cooperative drivers
  • Price locked when you confirm

Highlights

  • Chiang Mai National Museum's Lanna art collection
  • Ancient Buddha images and everyday artefacts
  • Wat Chet Yot, host of the 1477 World Buddhist Council
  • Stucco celestial figures around the seven-spired hall

  3. 11:00Wat Chet Yot (Wat Photharam Maha Wihan)
