Ban Pa Miang Valley Village, Lampang

A community-run Lampang valley village: stream-side homes, coffee gardens and a waterfall reached with locals

  • Free cancellation 24 h
  • Hotel pick-up
  • Licensed cooperative drivers
  • Drivers receive 60%

Highlights

  • Ban Pa Miang, a slow-paced community tourism village by a stream
  • Chae Son subdistrict, one of Lampang's key coffee-growing areas
  • Three-tier Tat Phai Waterfall by village truck with a local guide
  • Bauhinia blossom season, featured on TAT's flower routes

Programme

  1. 08:00Hotel pick-up
  2. 09:30Over the mountains via Mae Kampong
  3. 10:30Ban Pa Miang: village walk and local coffee
  4. 12:00Lunch in the village
  5. 13:00Tat Phai Waterfall (village truck + local guide)
  6. 15:00Stream-side café break
  7. 15:45Depart
  8. 18:00Back to your hotel

Drivers do not act as tour guides. A licensed guide can be added when you book.

Included

  • Pick-up and drop-off within the service area

  • Compulsory vehicle insurance
  • Free cancellation up to 24 hours before

Paid on site

The village truck and local guide to Tat Phai Waterfall are paid to the community at its posted rates · The falls are still being developed and the route may close after heavy rain · Food and drinks paid as you go · Nights and early mornings are very cold, so pack a jacket

Best time

Nov–Feb for crisp, cool days · The Bauhinia blossom season in late winter brings a village festival · Postpone in heavy rain
