Ob Luang Canyon & Bo Kaeo Pine Forest

Cross the suspension bridge over Ob Luang's gorge on the Mae Chaem River, then wander Bo Kaeo's pine forest

  • Free cancellation 24 h
  • Hotel pick-up
  • Licensed cooperative drivers
  • Price locked when you confirm

Highlights

  • A gorge about 300 m long, walls so close you can almost touch both
  • A suspension bridge and cliff-edge boardwalk over rushing water
  • Bo Kaeo's stately three-needle pines, over 40 years old
  • Highway 108 through the hills and farmland of Hot district

Programme

  1. 07:30Hotel pick-up
  2. 09:45Ob Luang National Park suspension bridge and gorge walk
  3. 11:15Short nature trail along the canyon
  4. 12:00Lunch on Highway 108
  5. 13:00Bo Kaeo pine forest stroll
  6. 14:15Head back
  7. 17:30Back to your hotel

Drivers do not act as tour guides. A licensed guide can be added when you book.

Paid on site

Ob Luang National Park entry at posted rates (sources cite 100–200 THB for foreigners), open about 08:00–16:30 · Parts of the bridge and platforms have no railings and rock is slick after rain · No shops inside the gorge trail, so carry water · Any Bo Kaeo fee at posted rates

Best time

Nov–Feb for clear jade water and cool pine air · Jun–Oct for a thundering river, but slippery paths
