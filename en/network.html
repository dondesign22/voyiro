The voyiro network

One standard across Thailand and ASEAN

Local public vehicles differ from place to place, but passengers, hotels and tour companies get the same experience in every city: easy to book, clear fares and licensed drivers — all to the voyiro standard.

Many vehicles, one standard

Different places, different vehicles, the same standard

voyiro doesn't replace each city's local vehicles. We connect them into one network, with standards passengers can trust anywhere.

The same uniform for everyone

voyiro drivers and staff everywhere wear the indigo and marigold uniform with an ID card — instantly recognisable in every city.

Drivers licensed under local law

We only accept public drivers licensed under each country's law, through local cooperatives or associations.

The same transparent pricing

Fares shown before you book in every city, no haggling, and the same receipt format.

QR and LINE work in every city

Passengers don't need a new app when travelling to another province or country.

Multiple languages and currencies

Thai, English, Chinese and local languages. [Pending: ภาษาและสกุลเงินที่จะรองรับในแต่ละประเทศ]

Local partners as hosts

In every area, local cooperatives, driver associations, hotels and tour companies are our core partners.

The voyiro uniform

See indigo and marigold, know you can trust it

Every voyiro driver and staff member, in every region and country, wears the same standard uniform.

  • Indigo polo shirt with marigold collar trim and the voyiro logo on the chest and back
  • Driver ID card showing photo, name, ID number and home cooperative or association
  • Driver ID number matches what passengers see on screen
  • Every vehicle carries a voyiro sticker and its own QR code
For cities and government agencies

A system that helps cities manage local public transport

voyiro works with municipalities, provincial land transport offices and local tourism authorities.

  • Service maps and authorised pick-up points
  • Safety protocols and incident reporting channels
  • Usage reports for city transport planning (no personal data disclosed)
  • Public signage in three languages: ไทย · English · မြန်မာ
Contact our government liaison team
For tour companies and hotel groups

One account to book rides in every city on the network

Tour companies taking customers across provinces or countries can manage all their destination transport from a single account.

  • Pre-book vehicles to match your tour itinerary in every city we serve
  • Published per-vehicle prices you can build into packages
  • Consolidated monthly invoicing [Pending: สกุลเงินและเงื่อนไขการวางบิลข้ามประเทศ]
  • Hotel groups with multiple branches get centralised QR codes and reports
