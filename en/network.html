The voyiro network

One standard, across Thailand and ASEAN

Local public vehicles differ from place to place, but the voyiro standard is the same in every city: easy to book, a clear price, a legal driver. We are live in Chiang Mai today; other cities and neighbouring countries are part of the expansion plan.

Many kinds of vehicle, one standard

Different places, different vehicles, the same standard

voyiro does not change each city's local vehicles. We connect them into a single network, with a standard passengers can trust anywhere.

The same uniform for everyone

voyiro drivers and staff everywhere wear the indigo and marigold uniform with an ID card, so they're recognisable in any city.

Drivers who are legal in their country

We only take on public transport drivers licensed under the law of their own country, through a local cooperative or association.

The same transparent pricing

The price is shown before you book in every city, with no haggling and the same receipt format.

One account in every city

As the network reaches a new city, passengers can book with their existing account straight away, with no need to sign up again in each province.

Thai and English

The passenger screens, receipts and messages to drivers all work in Thai and English.

Local partners as hosts

In every area, the local cooperatives, driver associations, hotels and tour companies are the main partners.

Thailand

Starting in Chiang Mai, then expanding to plan

Right now voyiro is only operating in the North, in Chiang Mai. Other areas are part of the expansion plan and will open once there is a local cooperative working with us and the necessary licences are in place there.

Under way

Phase 1 · Chiang Mai (2026)

  • Northern Thailand: pilot in Chiang Mai province
  • Local vehicles: songthaew (the red trucks), tuk-tuk, samlor, van
  • A system for songthaew and samlor cooperatives, connected to LINE and the driver app
  • A three-month live trial with hotels
  • Pilot target of 50 vehicles on 10 routes
Expansion plan

Phase 2 · Tourist cities in Thailand (2027–2028)

  • Cities in the plan: Phuket, Krabi, Pattaya, Hat Yai, Chiang Rai, Ayutthaya, Sukhothai
  • Adding vehicle types to suit each city
  • Not yet open; we are preparing partnerships with local cooperatives
Target

Three-year target

  • A target of more than 50 cities (a target, not areas we currently serve)
  • The same standard everywhere: uniform, safety, and Thai/English
  • Followed by phase 3, expansion into neighbouring countries

The voyiro uniform

See indigo and marigold, and you know you can trust it

Every voyiro driver and staff member, in every area we serve, wears the same standard uniform.

  • An indigo polo shirt with a marigold collar trim and the voyiro logo on the chest and back
  • A driver ID card showing their photo, name, code and the cooperative or association they belong to
  • A driver code that matches the one shown on the passenger's screen
  • Every vehicle has a vehicle code matching the registration shown to the passenger before they get in
For cities and government agencies

A system that helps cities look after local public transport

voyiro works with municipalities, provincial transport offices and local tourism bodies.

  • A map of the service and authorised pick-up points
  • Safety protocols and a channel for reporting incidents
  • Usage reports to support city travel planning (with no personal data disclosed)
  • Service information for passengers in two languages, ไทย · English

For tour companies and hotel groups

One account books vehicles in every city on the network

Tour companies taking customers across several provinces can manage all their vehicles at destination from a single account, growing as the network opens in each phase.

  • Book vehicles in advance to match the tour programme, in every city where the service is open
  • A charter price per vehicle published in advance that you can build into a package
  • A single monthly invoice, with a tax invoice
  • A 20 THB per trip share on tours and packages, with a payment record every cycle
  • Hotel groups can use a separate partner code per branch while still seeing central reports
