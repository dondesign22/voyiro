Khao Soi Trail: Three Legends

Three celebrated bowls of khao soi, Mae Sai, Khun Yai and Lamduan Fa Ham, to find your favourite broth

  • Free cancellation 24 h
  • Hotel pick-up
  • Licensed cooperative drivers
  • Drivers receive 60%

Highlights

  • Khao Soi Mae Sai, recommended by the Michelin Guide
  • Khao Soi Khun Yai, a tiny shop with short hours that sells out fast
  • Khao Soi Lamduan Fa Ham, a veteran on the east bank of the Ping
  • Share bowls so you can taste all three without overdoing it

Programme

  1. 10:00Hotel pick-up
  2. 10:20Khao Soi Mae Sai, Chang Phueak
  3. 11:05Khao Soi Khun Yai, Sri Poom Road
  4. 11:50Khao Soi Lamduan Fa Ham, Fa Ham
  5. 12:40Chilli dips and snacks to take home, on the way back
  6. 13:00Back to your hotel

Drivers do not act as tour guides. A licensed guide can be added when you book.

Paid on site

Food paid on site at posted prices · Runs Mon–Sat only (Mae Sai and Khun Yai close on Sundays) · Mae Sai 08:00–16:00 · Khun Yai 10:00–14:00, often sold out earlier · Lamduan Fa Ham approx. 08:30–15:30 daily

Best time

Monday to Saturday, 10:00–13:00, before shops sell out · Delicious year-round, best of all in the cool Nov–Feb months
