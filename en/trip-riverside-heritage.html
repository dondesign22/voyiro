Ping Riverside & Teak-Era Heritage

Teak-trade and railway-era Chiang Mai: Wat Ket's museum, Charoenrat Road, the station and the Foreign Cemetery

  • Free cancellation 24 h
  • Hotel pick-up
  • Licensed cooperative drivers
  • Price locked when you confirm

Highlights

  • Wat Ket museum's treasure trove of riverside life
  • Old timber houses and shophouses on Charoenrat Road
  • Chiang Mai Railway Station, end of the Northern Line
  • The Foreign Cemetery, on land granted by King Rama V in 1898

Programme

  4. 11:00Khua Lek iron footbridge
  5. 11:30Chiang Mai Railway Station
  6. 12:00Chiang Mai Foreign Cemetery
