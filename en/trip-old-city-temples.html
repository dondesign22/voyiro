Old City Temple Loop

Loop the old city by tuk-tuk or red truck to five landmark temples, from the great chedi to the silver chapel

  • Free cancellation 24 h
  • Hotel pick-up
  • Licensed cooperative drivers
  • Drivers receive 60%

Highlights

  • Wat Chedi Luang, the colossal brick stupa at the heart of the old city
  • Wat Phra Singh and its gilded Viharn Lai Kham
  • Wat Lok Moli, an ancient Lanna brick chedi
  • Wat Sri Suphan, the chapel clad entirely in silver

Programme

  1. 09:00Hotel pick-up
  2. 09:15Wat Lok Moli
  3. 09:50Wat Chedi Luang and neighbouring Wat Phan Tao
  4. 10:50Three Kings Monument (photo stop)
  5. 11:10Wat Phra Singh
  6. 12:00Wat Sri Suphan, Wua Lai silver quarter
  7. 13:00Back to your hotel

Drivers do not act as tour guides. A licensed guide can be added when you book.

Included

  • Pick-up and drop-off within the service area
  • Your driver waits at every stop on the programme

  • Compulsory vehicle insurance

Paid on site

Entry for foreign visitors: Wat Chedi Luang 50, Wat Phra Singh 50, Wat Sri Suphan 50 THB per person, paid on site · Wat Lok Moli and Wat Phan Tao are free · Cover shoulders and knees · Women may not enter the Wat Sri Suphan ordination hall

Best time

Mornings 09:00–13:00, before the midday heat · Coolest and clearest Nov–Feb
