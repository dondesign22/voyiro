Moat Loop: Five Gates & Four Bastions

Circle the 700-year-old walls with photo stops at all five gates and four corner bastions, ending at Tha Phae

  • Free cancellation 24 h
  • Hotel pick-up
  • Licensed cooperative drivers
  • Drivers receive 60%

Highlights

  • Jaeng Si Phum, the best-preserved corner bastion
  • Chang Phueak, Suan Dok and Saen Pung gates
  • Chiang Mai Gate and the Katam corner
  • Tha Phae Gate in the late-afternoon light

Programme

  1. 16:00Hotel pick-up
  2. 16:10Jaeng Si Phum (north-east corner)
  3. 16:25Chang Phueak Gate
  4. 16:40Jaeng Hua Lin · Suan Dok Gate
  5. 17:00Jaeng Ku Hueang · Saen Pung Gate
  6. 17:20Chiang Mai Gate · Jaeng Katam
  7. 17:40Tha Phae Gate
  8. 18:00Back to your hotel

Drivers do not act as tour guides. A licensed guide can be added when you book.
