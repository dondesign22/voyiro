Why drivers choose voyiro

Requirements

Who can apply

  • A member of a transport cooperative partnered with voyiro
  • A valid public driving licence or driver ID document for your vehicle type
  • A vehicle correctly registered for its type, with compulsory insurance and road tax up to date
  • Passes a background check [Pending: วิธีตรวจประวัติอาชญากรรม และหน่วยงานที่ตรวจ]
  • A smartphone that runs LINE; for cars, Android [Pending: เวอร์ชันขั้นต่ำ / รองรับ iPhone หรือไม่]

Guide to accepting jobs

How a trip works

Go available

While you're available, the app shares your vehicle location every 20 seconds.

Accept within 15 seconds

See the pick-up, drop-off, fare and payment method before you accept.

Pick up and confirm the passenger

Tap "Arrived", check the passenger's name, then start the trip.

Drop off and complete

Collect payment or confirm it's been paid, and the receipt is issued automatically.

Driver standards

One standard for every vehicle, in every province

These standards protect the reputation of every driver on the network.

  • Wear the voyiro uniform and driver ID card while on jobs — the same standard in every region and country
  • Random selfie identity checks 2–5 times a day; never let anyone else use your account
  • Charge only the fare shown — no extra charges
  • Never take passengers to shops they didn't ask to visit
  • Three-strike system: 1st — warning · 2nd — temporary suspension [Pending: กี่วัน] · 3rd — reviewed jointly by the cooperative and voyiro
FAQ

Questions from drivers

Can I still pick up passengers on the street?

Yes, as usual. voyiro is extra work — you're never required to take jobs only through the system.

Can I apply if I'm not a cooperative member?

For now we only accept drivers through participating cooperatives, so everyone on the network is licensed and supported.

How many hours a day do I have to drive?

There's no minimum. Go available whenever you're ready.

What if there's an accident during a job?

[Pending: ขั้นตอนเมื่อเกิดอุบัติเหตุ ประกันที่คุ้มครอง และเบอร์ติดต่อ]

Who can see a driver's personal information?

During a trip, passengers see only your name, photo, licence plate and licence number. Phone numbers are masked. [Pending: ยืนยันระบบซ่อนเบอร์โทร]
