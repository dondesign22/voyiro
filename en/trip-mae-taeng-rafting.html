Mae Taeng White-Water Rafting

Raft grade 3–4 rapids on the Mae Taeng River with a professional crew, then lunch at the riverside camp

  • Free cancellation 24 h
  • Hotel pick-up
  • Licensed cooperative drivers
  • Drivers receive 60%

Highlights

  • Grade 3–4 rapids on one of the north's best-known runs
  • Helmets, life jackets and a full safety briefing from the operator
  • Jungle-clad hills on both banks of the river
  • Transfer to the camp, with your driver waiting to bring you back

Programme

  1. 07:30Hotel pick-up
  2. 09:00Your booked rafting camp: change and gear up
  3. 09:30Operator's safety briefing
  4. 10:00Rafting the Mae Taeng River, distance per package
  5. 12:30Shower, change and lunch at camp
  6. 13:45Depart the camp
  7. 15:30Back to your hotel

Drivers do not act as tour guides. A licensed guide can be added when you book.

Included

  • Pick-up and drop-off within the service area
  • Your driver waits at every stop on the programme
  • Drinking water and a multilingual route card
  • Compulsory vehicle insurance
  • Free cancellation up to 24 hours before

Paid on site

Rafting paid directly to the operator (usually including gear and lunch); book ahead · Operators set minimum ages and may cancel or shorten runs in flood or low water · Your driver is not a rafting guide · Bring a change of clothes, strapped sandals and a dry bag

Best time

Jul–Sep for big water and bigger thrills · Oct–Feb for lower water and cool weather, ideal for first-timers · Confirm river levels with your operator
