Dawn Alms & Quiet Temples

Offer alms to monks near Chiang Mai Gate at sunrise, then Wat Phra Singh and Wat Chedi Luang before the crowds

  • Free cancellation 24 h
  • Hotel pick-up
  • Licensed cooperative drivers
  • Price locked when you confirm

Highlights

  • Buy a ready-made alms set at Chiang Mai Gate market
  • Offer alms as monks walk their morning round
  • Wat Phra Singh in the hush of early morning
  • Wat Chedi Luang before the tour groups arrive

Programme

  1. 06:00Hotel pick-up
  2. 06:15Chiang Mai Gate market: buy an alms set
  3. 06:30Morning alms offering
  4. 07:15Wat Phra Singh
  5. 07:50Wat Chedi Luang
  6. 08:30Back to your hotel

Drivers do not act as tour guides. A licensed guide can be added when you book.

Paid on site

Alms sets at vendor prices, paid yourself · Temple hall fees at posted rates (some buildings open after 08:00) · Remove shoes while offering; women should not touch monks · Add a guide if you'd like the etiquette explained

Best time

Daily, 06:00–08:30 · Chilly mornings Nov–Feb, bring a layer
