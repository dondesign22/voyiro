Two Ancient Cities in One Day

Lamphun's Hariphunchai stupa, Lampang's walled Lampang Luang temple and a horse-carriage ride, in one day

  • Free cancellation 24 h
  • Hotel pick-up
  • Licensed cooperative drivers
  • Drivers receive 60%

Highlights

  • Wat Phra That Hariphunchai, birth-year stupa for the Rooster
  • Wat Phra That Lampang Luang, birth-year stupa for the Ox
  • Horse-carriage ride along the Wang River
  • Wat Phra Kaew Don Tao Suchadaram in Lampang town

Programme

  1. 07:30Hotel pick-up
  2. 08:15Wat Phra That Hariphunchai, Lamphun
  3. 09:15Drive to Lampang
  4. 10:45Wat Phra That Lampang Luang
  5. 12:15Lunch
  6. 13:30Wat Phra Kaew Don Tao Suchadaram
  7. 14:30Horse-carriage ride
  8. 15:30Depart
  9. 17:30Back to your hotel

Drivers do not act as tour guides. A licensed guide can be added when you book.

Paid on site

Small foreigner entry fees at the temples at posted rates (Hariphunchai about 20 THB) · Carriages priced per carriage at the province's posted rates · Cover shoulders and knees; some halls are closed to women, as signposted

Best time

Year-round · Ideal if you have just one day for both provinces · Nov–Feb is coolest
