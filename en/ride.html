Frequently asked questions

Questions from passengers

How can I book a ride?

Through LINE chat or the voyiro app, in the Chiang Mai pilot in 2026.

Is it more expensive than hailing a vehicle in the street?

Fares are calculated from the published rates and shown before every booking. The partner's share comes out of that same fare — nothing extra is charged to the passenger.

How do I know the driver is licensed?

Every driver holds a public driving licence, every vehicle is properly registered, and the cooperative certifies its own members. Your screen shows the driver's name, driver ID and vehicle plate before you get in. See Safety and the law

What if I leave something in the vehicle?

Report the lost item from your trip receipt, and the system will contact the driver and their home cooperative for you.

Which languages are supported?

Thai and English.

How is passengers' personal data handled?

It is handled to PDPA standards and used only as far as necessary to provide the service.
