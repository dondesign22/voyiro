How to ride

Choose whatever's easiest for you

Scan a QR code

Find QR codes at hotel front desks, restaurants, attractions or on the vehicle itself. Just open your phone camera and scan.

LINE

Add LINE [Pending: LINE OA ID] as a friend and tap the ride menu. Works for Thai residents and travellers alike.

Ask your hotel

Tell the staff at your hotel or partner venue. They'll book the ride for you and send you the details.

Website

Book a ride on the web: [Pending: ลิงก์หน้าเรียกรถบนเว็บ เช่น voyiro.com/go และวันที่เปิดใช้]

Enter pick-up and drop-off

If you scan a hotel QR code, your pick-up point is filled in automatically.

Choose a vehicle

See the fare, estimated arrival time and number of seats.

Confirm and wait

The job goes to the nearest driver, who has 15 seconds to accept.

Check your driver before boarding

Make sure the name, photo, licence plate and licence number match your screen.

FAQ

Questions from passengers

Do I need to download an app?

No. Just scan a QR code or book through LINE straight away.

Is it more expensive than flagging a ride on the street?

Fares follow published rates and are always shown before you book. The partner share is included in the same fare — passengers are never charged extra.

How do I know my driver is licensed?

Your screen shows the driver's public driving licence number, licence plate and cooperative. See our standards at Safety and compliance.

Can I cancel? Is there a fee?

[Pending: นโยบายการยกเลิก เช่น ยกเลิกฟรีภายในกี่นาที ค่ายกเลิกเท่าไร]

What if I leave something in the vehicle?

Tap "Report lost item" on your receipt and we'll contact the driver and cooperative for you. [Pending: ค่าบริการส่งคืนของ ถ้ามี]

Which languages are supported?
