Frequently asked questions

Questions from passengers

How can I book a ride?

Through LINE chat or the voyiro app, in the Chiang Mai pilot in 2026.

Is it more expensive than other ride apps?

It is about the same. The fare is always shown before you book and locked when you confirm, so there is no haggling.

Why are fares higher at some times?

At night, in rush hour, or when more people are booking than there are drivers free, fares rise so that drivers come out to pick you up. The fare and the reason are always shown before you confirm. In quiet periods fares may drop.

Do I pay more if my hotel books for me?

There may be a service fee for booking through a hotel. It is always shown on its own line before you confirm.

Can I book a fixed-route songthaew through voyiro?

Yes, on routes the cooperative opens for booking. You pay the official fare in cash to the driver, and voyiro charges nothing extra.

How do I know the driver is licensed?

Every driver holds a public driving licence, every vehicle is properly registered, and the cooperative certifies its own members. Your screen shows the driver's name, driver ID and vehicle plate before you get in. See Safety and the law

What if I leave something in the vehicle?

Report the lost item from your trip receipt, and the system will contact the driver and their home cooperative for you.

Which languages are supported?

Thai and English.

How is passengers' personal data handled?

It is handled to PDPA standards and used only as far as necessary to provide the service.
