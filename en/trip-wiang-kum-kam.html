Wiang Kum Kam by Horse Cart

King Mangrai's lost city, buried by river silt for centuries, toured by horse cart, then a riverside café

  • Free cancellation 24 h
  • Hotel pick-up
  • Licensed cooperative drivers
  • Price locked when you confirm

Highlights

  • Wat Chedi Liam and its distinctive tiered stupa
  • A horse-cart ride through ruins scattered among village lanes
  • Wat Chang Kham (Kan Thom) and King Mangrai's shrine
  • Slow down at a café on the Ping River

Programme

  1. 08:30Hotel pick-up
  2. 09:00Wat Chedi Liam
  3. 09:30Wiang Kum Kam Information Centre · horse cart
  4. 10:30Wat Chang Kham (Kan Thom)
  5. 11:15Riverside café on the Ping
  6. 12:30Back to your hotel

Drivers do not act as tour guides. A licensed guide can be added when you book.
