Muay Thai Fight Night

Return transfers to live Muay Thai in central Chiang Mai, with your driver waiting until the final bout

  • Free to cancel until a driver accepts
  • Hotel pick-up
  • Licensed cooperative drivers
  • See the price before you book
  • Pay the driver in cash

Highlights

  • Live Muay Thai Monday to Saturday
  • The wai khru ram muay ritual dance before each bout
  • Choose Thapae or Loi Kroh stadium to suit your date
  • Your driver waits until the last bout for a safe late ride home

Programme

  1. 20:00Hotel pick-up
  2. 20:20Thapae Boxing Stadium or Loi Kroh Boxing Stadium (as booked)
  3. 21:00First bout; a card of about 6–10 fights
  4. 23:15Your driver waits outside the stadium
  5. 23:30Back to your hotel

Drivers do not act as tour guides.

The more of you, the better the value

Tuk-tuk with 2฿575 / person
Tuk-tuk with 3฿384 / person

Included and conditions

  • Private charter of the whole vehicle, no guide: up to 10 people in a songthaew, up to 3 in a tuk-tuk
  • Trip times are as shown for each trip
  • Pay the driver in cash
  • Hotel pickup and drop-off inside the old city moat; an extra fee applies outside the moat
  • Route and add-ons can be adjusted to suit you
  • Price does not include entry fees, meals or special activities (as listed)
  • Book ahead, especially for rafting and restaurants for large groups
  • Drinking water on board for every trip
  • Suitable for families, older travellers and every kind of visitor
  • No animal riding, shows or elephant bathing in our tours

Paid on site

Tickets paid at the stadium's posted rates, by seating tier · Thapae fights Mon–Sat · Loi Kroh Mon, Wed and Fri · Most stadiums are dark on Sundays

Best time

Monday to Saturday, 20:00–23:30 · Check the fight card before booking
