Samoeng Mountain Loop

The full Samoeng loop: mountain passes, Samoeng town, Pang Da royal station and the forest viewpoint

  • Free cancellation 24 h
  • Hotel pick-up
  • Licensed cooperative drivers
  • Drivers receive 60%

Highlights

  • Route 1269 over forested passes and hill villages
  • Samoeng's market town, tucked into a quiet valley
  • Pang Da Royal Agricultural Station's flower and fruit plots
  • Samoeng Forest Viewpoint at about 1,120 m

Programme

  1. 08:00Hotel pick-up, heading out via Hang Dong
  2. 09:00Ridge-top viewpoints on Route 1269
  3. 10:15Samoeng town and market
  4. 11:15Pang Da Royal Agricultural Station
  5. 12:30Lunch in Samoeng
  6. 13:45Samoeng Forest Viewpoint on Route 1096
  7. 14:45Strawberry farm (in season) or a Mae Sa Valley café
  8. 17:00Return via Mae Rim to your hotel

Drivers do not act as tour guides. A licensed guide can be added when you book.

Included

  • Pick-up and drop-off within the service area
  • Your driver waits at every stop on the programme
  • Drinking water and a multilingual route card
  • Compulsory vehicle insurance
  • Free cancellation up to 24 hours before

Paid on site

Most stops are free · Pang Da station open about 08:30–16:30, any fee at posted rates · Seasonal produce and meals paid on site · The road winds constantly, so bring motion-sickness tablets if needed

Best time

Nov–Feb for clear skies, cool air and strawberries · Jul–Oct for lush green hills, though cloud can hide the views
