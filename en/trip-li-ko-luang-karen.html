Ko Luang Turquoise Falls & Huai Tom Karen Village

Swim below emerald limestone falls in far southern Lamphun, then visit a Karen village and its golden chedi

  • Free cancellation 24 h
  • Hotel pick-up
  • Licensed cooperative drivers
  • Price locked when you confirm

Highlights

  • Ko Luang Waterfall in Mae Ping National Park: seven limestone tiers
  • Wat Phra Phutthabat Huai Tom, heart of the Pga K'nyau (Karen) community
  • Phra Maha That Chedi Si Wiang Chai, a golden chedi ringed by smaller stupas
  • A village shaped by its founding abbot's no-killing, vegetarian tradition

Programme

  1. 06:00Hotel pick-up
  2. 09:00Ko Luang Waterfall, Mae Ping National Park
  3. 11:30Lunch in Li
  4. 12:45Wat Phra Phutthabat Huai Tom
  5. 14:15Phra Maha That Chedi Si Wiang Chai
  6. 15:00Depart
  7. 18:00Back to your hotel

Drivers do not act as tour guides. A licensed guide can be added when you book.
