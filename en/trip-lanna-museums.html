Three Museums of Lanna

Seven centuries of Chiang Mai in three museums around the Three Kings Monument, the old city's civic heart

  • Free cancellation 24 h
  • Hotel pick-up
  • Licensed cooperative drivers
  • Price locked when you confirm

Highlights

  • The Three Kings Monument, heart of the old city
  • City Arts & Cultural Centre in a 1920s government hall
  • Lanna Folklife Museum's life-size village scenes
  • Historical Centre, with excavated ruins on its lower floor

Programme

  1. 09:00Hotel pick-up
  2. 09:15Three Kings Monument
  3. 09:30Chiang Mai City Arts & Cultural Centre
  4. 10:40Lanna Folklife Museum
  5. 11:40Chiang Mai Historical Centre
  6. 12:30Back to your hotel
