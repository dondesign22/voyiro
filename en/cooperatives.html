Today's challenge

Passengers have moved to booking rides on their phones

Tourists and younger travellers are used to booking a ride through an app, with a clear price and live tracking. Cooperatives without a system of their own are losing those passengers to vehicles outside the public transport system.

voyiro helps cooperative members

  • Accept jobs on a phone, just like the big apps
  • Reach passengers from local hotels, tour companies and restaurants
  • Show a clear price, reducing haggling and complaints
  • Build a modern, trustworthy image for local public transport

Local public transport drivers are earning less as they compete with private vehicles taking jobs through apps.

What the cooperative gets

The cooperative still owns its members and its routes

You approve your own members

Drivers can only enter the system once the cooperative approves them, and the cooperative can suspend them immediately.

Cooperative dashboard

See every member vehicle in real time, the jobs they accept and any complaints.

Revenue share for the cooperative

The cooperative earns a share of every trip its members take through the system. The percentage is agreed together in the partnership agreement and can go towards member welfare.

Jobs from partners

voyiro brings in local hotels, tour companies and restaurants as a source of passengers for your members.

Assigning tour work

The cooperative assigns drivers for full-day charters, following its own queue and its own rules.

The cooperative's data

Member data and trip statistics belong to the cooperative. This is stated in the partnership agreement, and the data is handled to PDPA standards.

Frequently asked questions

Questions from cooperatives

Will voyiro take on drivers outside the cooperative to compete with our members?

No. voyiro only accepts public transport drivers who belong to a participating cooperative.

What does it cost the cooperative?

During the pilot there is no joining fee and no charge for using the system. Terms after the pilot will be agreed together before the trial ends.

Can we end the partnership if we want to?

Yes. The termination terms, the notice period and the return of member data are all set out in the partnership agreement signed before you start.

What about members who aren't comfortable with smartphones?

The voyiro team runs training at the cooperative, and the system can take jobs through LINE, which members already use.

Can driver associations in other countries take part?

Yes. Phase 3, from 2029, is expansion into ASEAN, starting with Laos, Cambodia and Vietnam. Contact the team to discuss a partnership model that complies with that country's law.

Can several cooperatives in the same province use it?

Yes. The system keeps each cooperative's members, reports and revenue share separate.
