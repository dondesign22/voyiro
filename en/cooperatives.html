Today's challenge

Passengers now book rides on their phones

Travellers and younger riders are used to booking through apps, with clear prices and live tracking. Cooperatives without their own system are losing passengers to vehicles outside the public transport system.

voyiro helps cooperative members

  • Accept jobs on their phones, just like the big apps
  • Get passengers from local hotels, tour operators and restaurants
  • Offer clear prices, with less haggling and fewer complaints
  • Build a modern, trustworthy image for public transport

What your cooperative gets

Your cooperative still owns its members and routes

Approve your own members

Drivers can only join once the cooperative approves them, and the cooperative can suspend them instantly.

Cooperative dashboard

See every member vehicle in real time, along with jobs accepted and complaints.

Revenue share for the cooperative

The cooperative receives [Pending: สัดส่วนหรือจำนวนเงินที่สหกรณ์ได้ต่อเที่ยว], which can fund member benefits.

FAQ

Questions from cooperatives

Will voyiro bring in drivers from outside the cooperative to compete with members?

No. voyiro only accepts licensed public drivers who belong to a participating cooperative.

What does it cost the cooperative?

There's no charge to use the system during the pilot. [Pending: ค่าใช้จ่ายหลังนำร่อง ถ้ามี]

Can we end the partnership if we want to?

[Pending: เงื่อนไขการยกเลิก ระยะเวลาแจ้งล่วงหน้า และการคืนข้อมูลสมาชิก]

What about members who aren't comfortable with smartphones?

The voyiro team runs training at your cooperative, and jobs come through LINE, which members already use.

Can driver associations in other countries join?

Yes. voyiro is planning to expand to Laos, Myanmar, Malaysia and other ASEAN countries. Contact our team to discuss a partnership model that complies with local law.

Can several cooperatives in the same province use it?

Yes. The system keeps each cooperative's members, reports and revenue shares separate.
