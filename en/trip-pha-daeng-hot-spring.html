Pong Arng Hot Springs & Si Sangwan Falls

Soak in Pong Arng's forest hot springs in Pha Daeng National Park, then climb Si Sangwan's limestone falls

  • Free cancellation 24 h
  • Hotel pick-up
  • Licensed cooperative drivers
  • Price locked when you confirm

Highlights

  • Natural hot pools stepping down from scalding to soothing
  • Si Sangwan Waterfall, about 20 m of grippy limestone you can climb
  • Quiet forest visited mostly by local Thai families
  • One park ticket covers both sites

Programme

  1. 07:30Hotel pick-up
  2. 09:45Pha Daeng National Park: Si Sangwan Waterfall
  3. 11:00Pong Arng Hot Springs soak
  4. 12:30Lunch in Chiang Dao town
  5. 14:00Roadside viewpoint of Doi Luang Chiang Dao
  6. 16:30Back to your hotel

Drivers do not act as tour guides. A licensed guide can be added when you book.

  • Free cancellation up to 24 hours before

Paid on site

Pha Daeng National Park entry at posted rates, one ticket for the falls and springs, plus parking · No restaurants inside the park, so buy water and snacks in town · Bathe fully clothed (bikinis are inappropriate here) · Stay out of the scalding source pools; basic showers and changing rooms on site

Best time

Nov–Feb, when cool air makes the hot pools perfect · Rainy season brings strong falls and slippery paths
