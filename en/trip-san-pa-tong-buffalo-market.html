San Pa Tong Saturday Buffalo Market

Watch farmers trade buffalo at dawn, eat a northern breakfast, then visit Wiang Tha Kan and Wat Ton Kwen

  • Free cancellation 24 h
  • Hotel pick-up
  • Licensed cooperative drivers
  • Drivers receive 60%

Highlights

  • Kad Wua Kwai Ban Thung Fa Bot, a Saturday-morning livestock market
  • Street food, second-hand goods and farm tools
  • Wiang Tha Kan, a moated Hariphunchai-era town among rice fields
  • Wat Ton Kwen, a Lanna wooden temple hall built in 1858

Programme

  1. 05:30Hotel pick-up
  2. 06:15Ban Thung Fa Bot buffalo market, San Pa Tong
  3. 08:00Northern Thai breakfast at the market
  4. 09:00Wiang Tha Kan ruins, moat and small museum
  5. 10:30Wat Ton Kwen (Wat Intharawat), Hang Dong
  6. 11:30Back to your hotel

Drivers do not act as tour guides. A licensed guide can be added when you book.

Paid on site

Market runs Saturdays only, about 05:00–11:00, free entry · Wiang Tha Kan and Wat Ton Kwen are free (donations welcome) · The market ground is dusty and muddy, so wear closed shoes you don't mind soiling · Ask before photographing people · Dress modestly for the temple

Best time

Saturdays only; arrive before 08:00 for the liveliest trading · Nov–Feb for cool, misty mornings over the fields
