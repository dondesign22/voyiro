Thep Sadet Coffee Highlands

Head up into Doi Saket's cool coffee hills for a farm visit, bean-to-cup tasting and forest cafés

  • Free cancellation 24 h
  • Hotel pick-up
  • Licensed cooperative drivers
  • Drivers receive 60%

Highlights

  • Thep Sadet coffee, protected by a Thai Geographical Indication (GI)
  • Shade-grown arabica on slopes of 700–1,400 m
  • A forest café with a suspension bridge into the treetops
  • Cool mountain air for most of the year

Programme

  1. 08:00Hotel pick-up
  2. 09:30Up into the Thep Sadet hills
  3. 10:00Community coffee farm (pre-arranged): see, roast, taste
  4. 12:00Lunch in the hills
  5. 13:15Forest café with suspension bridge
  6. 14:30Viewpoint café over the Doi Saket ranges
  7. 15:30Depart
  8. 17:00Back to your hotel

Drivers do not act as tour guides. A licensed guide can be added when you book.

Included

  • Pick-up and drop-off within the service area
  • Your driver waits at every stop on the programme
  • Drinking water and a multilingual route card
  • Compulsory vehicle insurance
  • Free cancellation up to 24 hours before

Paid on site

Farm visit or roasting session paid to the farm at its posted rates (book ahead) · Food and drinks paid as you go · The mountain road is long and winding, so bring motion-sickness tablets if needed · Popular cafés have weekend queues

Best time

Nov–Feb: harvest season and cool weather · Weekdays are quieter · Roads are slippery in heavy rain
