Lanna Timber Temples of Ko Kha

Old Lanna temples of Lampang: walled Lampang Luang, an upside-down chedi image and wooden Wat Pong Yang Khok

  • Free cancellation 24 h
  • Hotel pick-up
  • Licensed cooperative drivers
  • Price locked when you confirm

Highlights

  • Wat Phra That Lampang Luang, a walled temple and the birth-year stupa for the Ox
  • A camera-obscura image of the chedi, projected upside down in a darkened hall
  • Wat Pong Yang Khok, an old wooden viharn tied to Chamadevi-era legend
  • Wat Lai Hin and Wat Phra That Chom Ping in the Ko Kha countryside

Programme

  1. 07:30Hotel pick-up
  2. 09:15Wat Pong Yang Khok, Hang Chat
  3. 10:15Wat Phra That Lampang Luang, Ko Kha
  4. 12:15Lunch in Ko Kha
  5. 13:30Wat Lai Hin
  6. 14:30Wat Phra That Chom Ping: the inverted chedi image
  7. 15:30Depart
  8. 17:30Back to your hotel

Drivers do not act as tour guides. A licensed guide can be added when you book.
